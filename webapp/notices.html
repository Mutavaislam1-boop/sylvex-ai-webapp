    Legal Notices & Policy Updates

    Version: 0.3.0-editorial

    1. Policy versions

    SYLVEX policies explain different aspects of the service. Pricing, credit rules, privacy and content conditions may be updated separately, but a new page does not automatically replace the terms governing an earlier paid purchase. The applicable version, publication date and effective date identify an issued policy. Previous versions remain available for reference through support.

    2. Material changes

    We provide appropriate advance information about material changes to prices, renewal, paid access, credit treatment, permitted uses or important data-processing purposes. Notices may be sent to the account email, shown in the service, delivered through Telegram or provided by another legally required channel. Minor editorial corrections do not justify an undisclosed material change.

    Where renewed agreement is required, we identify the change and obtain explicit acceptance of the relevant version, recording the action and its date and time. Optional tracking and marketing choices remain separate. Refusing new terms does not remove mandatory data, complaint or remedy rights.

    3. Existing purchases and the temporary offer

    Already-paid rights are not reduced retrospectively, and granted credits are not removed merely to introduce a different plan structure. Changes must identify the affected purchases or renewals and the applicable transition.

    Pricing & Plans explains the temporary equal-access offer and future model-based access. Its transition requires prior disclosure of the actual rules and protection of paid rights. A change in model access does not automatically amend credit treatment.

    4. Service and security notices

    Maintenance, security incidents or lawful restrictions may require timely service notices. Where advance warning is not possible, an appropriate explanation is provided when permitted. Urgent action does not automatically remove remedies for paid service not supplied. Service notices are distinct from optional marketing.

    5. Languages and clarification

    English is the principal drafting language; Russian provides a full user version. Both express the same policy. English does not automatically override a valid translated undertaking or mandatory local-language and consumer rights.

    If you identify a discrepancy or need the version applicable to a purchase, contact support@sylvex.ai. Corrections must protect affected rights. An older translation is not automatically the current policy merely because it remains accessible.
