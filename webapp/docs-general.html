    User Documentation

    Version: 0.3.0-editorial

    1. Start with the right account

    On the website or Browser Mini App, register or sign in using an offered method, such as email or Google. In Telegram, open the official SYLVEX bot or its Mini App. You must be 18 or over and accept the required agreements before generation; optional privacy choices are separate.

    The website and Browser Mini App share the web account, balance and subscriptions. Telegram uses a separate system. Check your account and channel before purchasing or looking for saved work. There is no self-service merging; exceptional linking or transfer requires support approval.

    2. Check your plan and credits

    Open your profile to review your plan and available ⚡ balance. Paid AI operations require an active qualifying subscription and sufficient credits. Review the cost before starting, including any separately priced planning, transcription or other AI assistance. Annual included credits are granted upfront after payment confirmation.

    See Pricing & Plans for the five plans and temporary equal-access conditions, Credits & Subscriptions for charging, and Payments, Refunds & Cancellation for purchase management.

    3. Pro Studio

    Choose Image, Video, Text, Music or Voice and select an available model. Enter the prompt, attach supported inputs and review the controls for that model: output count, aspect ratio, resolution, duration, sound or other offered parameters. Capabilities and prices vary by model and action.

    • Image: choose the supported generation or editing workflow and check the selected output when several images are produced.
    • Video: choose the offered text, image, video-reference or motion workflow. Confirm supported references, duration and audio settings.
    • Text: use the selected conversation or tool with supported attachments. Model text tokens are different from SYLVEX credits. Review important claims.
    • Music: select the offered music, lyrics or audio workflow and use source material you are entitled to process.
    • Voice: choose a supported voice or tool. Voice cloning and identity use require appropriate permission; preview or transcription may involve paid processing.

    Submit once and follow the request's progress before retrying. Model selection does not override content or licensing rules.

    4. References, styles, characters and objects

    Use the upload or reference area for a supported file, permitted catalog asset or saved resource. Check format, size and duration requirements in the selected control. Attach each item to the intended slot: a video reference, character identity image and end frame serve different purposes.

    Where character or object selection is offered, select the resource explicitly. Check that it is attached before starting. Styles guide supported requests without guaranteeing exact reproduction. Obtain necessary rights for people's images, voices and other protected material.

    5. Grid Mode

    Create or open a project, add available nodes and connect compatible inputs and outputs. Check the model, references, dependencies and cost of each node before running. A chain may include several paid operations. AI-assisted planning can have its own charge.

    Review each result before continuing or retrying a node. A saved output can be reused without regenerating it; a new paid operation has its own cost. The temporary offer introduces no additional Grid-specific subscription limits; technical execution limits still apply.

    6. Edit and Video Editor

    In Edit, select a source and an offered action, such as retouching, removal, replacement, try-on, Camera, Lighting or Restore. Use the masks, references and settings provided for that action.

    In Video Editor, import permitted media, work with the available timeline and clip controls, and review export settings. Local editing is distinct from separately chargeable AI processing or transcription. Save important work and inspect the exported file before removing sources.

    7. Results, downloads and reuse

    Find available work in history, results or My Files. Cloud records and local drafts are different; local data can be lost if browser storage is cleared. Account content follows the Privacy Policy and Account & Data Deletion Policy. Download copies you need; backup recovery of every media file is not guaranteed.

    On a generation card, open the download picker and select one, several or all images. Each selected image downloads as a separate file. Your browser may request permission for multiple downloads. In an individual result view, check the selected image before downloading or reusing it as a reference. Reuse does not itself imply a new generation; a subsequent paid run is charged separately. Telegram delivery controls may differ.

    8. Getting help

    For a pending request, check its existing status before starting another generation. For access, payment or output questions, retain the relevant reference and follow Help & FAQ or Contact & Support. The payment policy explains correction of charged technical failures and recovery of existing results without a new generation charge.
