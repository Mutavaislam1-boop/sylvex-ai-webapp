    Telegram Addendum

    Version: 0.3.0-editorial

    1. Scope

    This addendum applies to the SYLVEX Telegram bot and Telegram Mini App, operated by SYLVEX AI, LLC independently of Telegram. Telegram's platform, privacy and Stars rules also apply to its services. The Terms of Use and related policies remain applicable; this addendum explains channel differences without reducing mandatory rights.

    2. Accounts

    The bot and Telegram Mini App use the Telegram account system, separate from the website and Browser Mini App. Balances, subscriptions and history do not merge automatically. A web purchase does not automatically fund Telegram or vice versa. Self-service merging is not available; exceptional linking or transfer requires support verification and approval and is not guaranteed. Existing legitimate rights and records remain protected.

    3. Purchases and periods

    Digital purchases within Telegram use Telegram Stars (XTR). Review the Stars amount, account, benefits and term on the invoice before confirming. Stars are distinct from SYLVEX ⚡ and model text tokens. Their acquisition cost may vary by channel and taxes or fees; no fixed dollar exchange or general cash redemption is promised. Benefits follow confirmed successful payment.

    Native recurring monthly subscriptions use 30-day periods, not calendar months. Annual Telegram offers are one-off purchases of 365-day access, without automatic annual renewal. Included annual credits are granted once upfront after confirmed payment; there are no additional monthly grants on top of that amount. Credits & Subscriptions governs credit treatment.

    4. Model access

    The temporary equal-access conditions in Pricing & Plans apply to all five plans. Paid generation requires active qualifying access and sufficient credits. Model capabilities, supported files, content rules and provider restrictions apply within Telegram as elsewhere. Future access changes require advance disclosure and protection of already-paid rights.

    5. Cancellation and plan changes

    Use the management controls for the recurring purchase and confirm cancellation and the paid end date. Blocking the bot, deleting a chat or closing a Mini App does not itself confirm cancellation. One-off annual purchases have no automatic annual renewal to cancel.

    After cancellation of renewal, paid access ordinarily remains until its stated end, subject to applicable withdrawal or termination rights. A plan change follows the timing, price and credit treatment offered before confirmation; an immediate prorated upgrade is not guaranteed. Contact support if controls or confirmation are unavailable.

    6. Support and remedies

    SYLVEX remains responsible for its delivery and payment-support obligations. Contact support@sylvex.ai with a safe account and invoice or operation reference. Telegram support does not replace the seller's responsibilities.

    Charged technical generation failures require credit correction. Existing inaccessible results should first be recovered without a new generation charge; if recovery is impossible, an appropriate remedy is due. Owed Stars refunds use Telegram's supported mechanism, not a promised fiat refund or arbitrary balance transfer. Mandatory consumer rights remain available under Payments, Refunds & Cancellation.

    7. Data and content

    Telegram processes its own platform records and provides relevant account and authentication information. SYLVEX processes submitted requests and media under its Privacy Policy. Deleting a Telegram chat does not itself erase all SYLVEX records or legally required financial evidence. Export needed results and use Account & Data Deletion for a scoped request.
