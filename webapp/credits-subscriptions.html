    Credits & Subscriptions Policy

    Version: 0.3.0-editorial

    1. Service credits

    ⚡ are internal SYLVEX credits used for paid service operations, with a nominal calculation value of 1 ⚡ = USD 0.01. They are distinct from Telegram Stars (XTR) and the text-processing tokens used by AI models. Credits are not offered as cryptocurrency or an investment, for user-to-user trading or for general voluntary cash withdrawal. Mandatory refund or redemption rights remain unaffected; legal treatment depends on the product and applicable law.

    2. Access and account balances

    Paid generation requires both an active qualifying subscription and enough available credits. A top-up adds credits but does not grant or extend subscription access. Subscription expiry does not by itself erase unused credits; renewed qualifying access is needed to use them for paid generation.

    The website and Browser Mini App share the web balance and subscriptions. Telegram has a separate balance and subscriptions. There is no automatic transfer or self-service merging. Exceptional transfers or links require support verification and approval and are not guaranteed.

    The five plans and the temporary equal-access offer are detailed in Pricing & Plans. Technical and lawful-use restrictions apply to every plan.

    3. Allocations and balance

    Subscription credits and top-ups appear in one displayed balance. Included annual credits are granted upfront after confirmed payment, at twelve times the plan's monthly allocation. There are no additional monthly grants on top of that annual amount.

    The balance does not automatically expire merely through the passage of time or the end of subscription access. Refunds, payment reversals and correction of mistakes are distinct adjustments governed by the purchase terms and applicable law. A subscription's paid end date is not a separate expiry date for each credit.

    4. How usage is charged

    The selected operation's price or disclosed estimate depends on the model and relevant settings, such as output count, resolution, duration, references or metered text and audio usage. Planning, transcription and other AI-assisted preparation may be separately chargeable; the applicable cost must be shown before use. Local actions without paid AI processing are not automatically generation charges.

    Where an estimate is reserved, that amount is temporarily unavailable for other spending. The final charge follows the disclosed calculation method and actual metered usage; the unused amount is released. A retry of the same completed operation must not create a duplicate charge.

    5. Corrections and changes

    A charged technical failure requires restoration of the corresponding credits. For an existing but inaccessible result, seek recovery without a new generation charge. Payments, Refunds & Cancellation explains remedies, including monetary refunds where required. Contact support@sylvex.ai with the relevant operation or purchase reference if you need a review.

    Future changes to credit treatment require advance disclosure, lawful application and protection of existing paid rights. Already-granted value is not withdrawn or retrospectively reclassified as expiring promotional credit merely to introduce a new plan structure. Changes to model access do not automatically change credit treatment. See Legal Notices & Policy Updates.
