    AI Acceptable Use Policy

    Version: 0.3.0-editorial

    1. Responsible use

    These rules apply to prompts, uploads, references, characters, voice samples, outputs and sharing across SYLVEX. A subscription or credit balance does not override legal, safety or model-provider restrictions.

    Use material you own, are licensed to use or may otherwise lawfully process. Obtain the rights and permissions appropriate to people's likenesses, voices, private information, music and other protected material. Public availability alone is not permission. Do not submit confidential or sensitive information you are not entitled to disclose to the selected processing service.

    2. Prohibited uses

    Do not use SYLVEX to:

    • Carry out illegal activity or evade applicable sanctions, territorial or provider restrictions.
    • Exploit children or create, solicit or distribute child sexual abuse material or sexualized depictions of minors.
    • Create or distribute non-consensual intimate imagery or sexual deepfakes, or facilitate coercion, extortion or threats.
    • Impersonate deceptively, fabricate identity or payment documents, conduct fraud, phishing or scams, or present synthetic evidence as authentic.
    • Infringe copyright, trademarks, privacy, publicity or other protected rights, including unlawful voice cloning or use of a person's likeness.
    • Facilitate serious violence or harm, malware, unauthorized access, abusive surveillance, doxxing or targeted harassment.
    • Bypass account security, safety measures, subscription requirements, payment controls, rate limits or other access restrictions.
    • Evade these rules through references, altered age labels, encoded instructions or other indirect methods.

    Applicable laws and model providers may impose additional restrictions. An output being generated does not establish that its intended use is lawful.

    3. Synthetic media and reliance

    Follow applicable disclosure, attribution and provenance requirements. Make AI involvement clear where the context could mislead others, and do not remove required marks or disclosures to facilitate deception. Requirements vary by use; a universal visible watermark is not imposed by this policy.

    Review important claims and outputs before use. Generated media is not proof of a real event, identity, consent or professional authorization. SYLVEX is not offered as a validated medical, legal, employment, credit or other high-risk decision system.

    4. Commercial use and sharing

    Commercial use depends on applicable law, input rights and the relevant model license, as explained in Intellectual Property & Copyright. No plan grants automatic exclusivity or unrestricted rights to every output.

    Share material only with the intended audience and appropriate permissions. Private generations do not become advertising material merely through use of SYLVEX; promotional use requiring permission needs separate permission and relevant rights.

    5. Enforcement and review

    We assess credible reports and may proportionately remove or restrict content or access. Ordinary confirmed breaches may lead to warnings; serious or repeated violations can require immediate action. Applicable notice, reasons, appeal and counter-notice rights remain available, subject to safety and lawful confidentiality limits. A report alone does not prove infringement.

    To seek review, identify the affected content or decision and explain the alleged mistake to Support. Restrictions do not remove mandatory data-request or remedy rights. Billing remedies follow Payments, Refunds & Cancellation.

    6. Reporting harm

    Email support@sylvex.ai with a location or identifier and a safe description. For child safety or intimate imagery, do not forward prohibited media. Copyright claims follow the IP Policy. For an immediate threat to life, contact the relevant emergency authority; SYLVEX is not an emergency service. We cannot remove copies from unrelated websites or other people's devices.
