    1. SYLVEX

    SYLVEX is an AI creative platform for working with images, video, text, music and voice. The web platform brings together Pro Studio for model-based creation, Grid Mode for connected workflows, and editing tools for supported creative tasks. Telegram services provide access through their own account and payment channel.

    SYLVEX integrates third-party AI services. Available capabilities depend on the selected model, applicable permissions and service availability. The User Documentation explains how to choose tools, work with references and manage results.

    3. Accounts and plans

    SYLVEX is for users aged 18 or over. The website and Browser Mini App share a web account, balance and subscriptions. The Telegram bot and Telegram Mini App use a separate system; paid benefits and history do not merge automatically.

    The five plans are Starter, Pro, Max, Ultra and Creator. See Pricing & Plans for prices, credits and the temporary equal-access offer. Paid generation requires an active qualifying subscription and sufficient ⚡ credits.
