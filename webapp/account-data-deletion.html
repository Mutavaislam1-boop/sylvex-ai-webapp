    Account & Data Deletion Policy

    Version: 0.3.0-editorial

    1. Your choices

    You may request deletion of particular content or your account. Removing content, closing account access, canceling renewal and requesting a refund have different effects. This policy explains the scope of erasure and lawful retention.

    The website and Browser Mini App share the web account; the Telegram bot and Telegram Mini App use a separate system. Identify which account and materials your request concerns. There is no automatic transfer of balance, subscription or history to a new account or another channel.

    2. Before account deletion

    Download work you wish to keep and review renewal in the purchase channel. Confirm cancellation separately: deleting an account or Telegram chat, or blocking the bot, does not by itself confirm that every external renewal has stopped. Contact support if you cannot access the relevant controls.

    Deletion does not automatically refund previous use. Applicable financial rights and legitimate paid value are addressed under Payments, Refunds & Cancellation. Existing authorized links or shared material may require clarification to avoid affecting another person's rights.

    3. Making a request

    Email support@sylvex.ai with a safe account reference, web or Telegram channel, and the scope: a copy, correction, particular material or account-level erasure. You can submit a lawful request while signed out or blocked, without making a purchase or accepting new Terms.

    We verify ownership proportionately and respond within applicable statutory periods. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes. The response explains actions taken, justified retained categories, any lawful extension or refusal and available review or complaint routes. Related rights and response periods are in the Privacy Policy.

    4. Scope of a verified deletion

    Account erasure covers access and relevant sessions, personal account details, creative history, uploads, results, projects, saved resources and linked files in active storage, except for specifically justified lawful retention. Deletion of a particular item is limited to that scope and must not remove unrelated work.

    We take applicable steps concerning processor-held copies and public links within our responsibility. Independent platforms, unrelated websites and copies already downloaded by others may remain outside our control. A shared resource requires assessment of the rights of other legitimate users.

    5. Lawful retention and financial matters

    Required payment, refund and accounting records remain for statutory periods. Specific incident or dispute evidence may be retained on a limited lawful basis. This does not authorize indefinite retention of every prompt, photo or result. Support records follow the period in the Privacy Policy, normally up to 12 months after closure, subject to its exceptions and applicable erasure rights.

    Pending paid work, subscription matters and owed remedies must be addressed under the purchase terms and law. Deletion is not a basis to confiscate legitimate value or alter unrelated users' records.

    6. Backups and completion

    Residual information in protected database backups may remain until scheduled removal, for no longer than 30 days. Access is restricted, and valid deletion decisions must be reapplied if a backup is restored. Separate legal records require their own retention basis rather than indefinite preservation of a full backup. Ordinary logs are retained for 30 days, with limited exceptions for specific incidents.

    Backups do not guarantee recovery of deleted media. If you believe a request is incomplete or has been wrongly refused, ask support for an explanation or review; applicable regulatory, complaint and judicial rights remain available.
