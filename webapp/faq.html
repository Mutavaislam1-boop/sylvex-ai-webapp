    Help & FAQ

    Version: 0.3.0-editorial

    1. Are web and Telegram accounts shared?

    The website and Browser Mini App share a web account, balance and subscriptions. The Telegram bot and Telegram Mini App use a separate system. Purchases and history do not automatically transfer. Self-service merging is not available; exceptional linking or transfer requires support verification and approval.

    2. How do the plans differ?

    Prices and included credits differ. During the temporary test offer, all five plans have equal access to technically available and permitted models and tools. Future model access will depend on subscription level, with advance disclosure and protection of already-paid rights. The full offer is in Pricing & Plans.

    3. Do I need a subscription if I have credits?

    Yes. Paid generation requires both active qualifying access and sufficient available ⚡. A top-up does not replace or extend a subscription. Ending access does not itself erase unused credits. See Credits & Subscriptions.

    4. When are annual credits granted?

    The annual allocation is granted once upfront after confirmed payment. It is twelve times the plan's monthly allocation, without further monthly grants on top. Credits appear in one balance.

    5. Which payment methods are offered?

    For purchases offered on the website or Browser Mini App, Stripe Checkout shows available payment methods, currency and the total before confirmation. Digital purchases within Telegram use Stars (XTR), a different unit from ⚡. Review the account and purchase summary before paying.

    6. What are the subscription periods?

    Web periods follow the confirmed monthly or annual subscription dates. Native recurring Telegram subscriptions run for 30 days; annual Telegram offers are one-off 365-day access purchases without automatic annual renewal.

    7. How do I cancel, change a plan or request a refund?

    Use subscription management in the purchase channel and confirm the recorded cancellation and paid end date. Plan changes follow the price, timing and credit treatment shown before confirmation. Cancellation, statutory withdrawal and refunds are different actions. Mandatory consumer remedies remain available; no general voluntary change-of-mind refund is offered. See Payments, Refunds & Cancellation for details and how to make a request.

    8. Where do I ask about a purchase or generation?

    Contact support with your receipt or operation reference and the relevant web or Telegram account. Check a pending request before submitting another purchase or generation. A charged technical failure requires credit correction; an existing but inaccessible result should first be recovered without a new generation charge. The payment policy explains remedies.

    9. How do I download or reuse images?

    Open the generation-card download picker and choose one, several or all images. Each selected image is a separate file. Your browser may ask permission for multiple downloads. In an individual result view, check the selected image before downloading or reusing it as a reference. See User Documentation.

    10. How are work and personal data handled?

    Selected external AI services process the inputs needed for your request. Account work is retained until you delete the relevant material or account, subject to specific lawful retention. The Privacy Policy lists retention periods and rights; Account & Data Deletion explains requests, backups and lawful exceptions. Download work you need before deletion.

    11. Can I use results commercially or clone a voice?

    Only within applicable law, input rights, necessary permissions and the selected model's license. Subscription payment does not guarantee exclusivity or unrestricted use. Follow AI Acceptable Use and Intellectual Property & Copyright.

    12. How do I reach support?

    Email support@sylvex.ai. Contact & Support explains useful information for a request, privacy-safe reporting and complaint routes.
