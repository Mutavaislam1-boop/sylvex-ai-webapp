    Payments, Refunds & Cancellation Policy

    Version: 0.3.0-editorial

    1. Payment methods

    For purchases offered on the website or Browser Mini App, Stripe Checkout handles payment processing. Available methods, currency and the total amount are shown before confirmation. Digital purchases within Telegram use Telegram Stars (XTR). Use the official checkout for the account in which you want the purchase benefits.

    This policy covers subscriptions, credit top-ups, cancellation and payment remedies. Restoration of service credits is distinct from, and does not replace, a monetary refund required by law.

    2. Purchase information and confirmation

    Before confirming, review the account, plan, included credits, term, currency, total amount including disclosed taxes or charges, and renewal method. Catalog prices are in Pricing & Plans. Stars are different from SYLVEX ⚡ credits; their acquisition cost may vary by channel, taxes and fees.

    Benefits are granted after successful payment confirmation by the payment platform. A redirect or screenshot alone does not confirm payment. Keep your receipt. If a confirmed purchase has not appeared in your account, contact support with the reference before buying again. A top-up adds credits but does not create or extend subscription access.

    3. Terms and renewal

    Web subscriptions use the monthly or annual period and renewal date confirmed in checkout. Native recurring Telegram subscriptions use 30-day periods. Telegram annual offers are one-off purchases of 365-day access, without automatic annual renewal. See the Telegram Addendum.

    A recurring offer identifies the recurring amount, frequency, next charge and cancellation method and requires any consent prescribed by law. Changes to renewal price or benefits require appropriate prior information, notice and any necessary consent. Paid rights are not retrospectively reduced.

    4. Canceling or changing a subscription

    Use subscription management in the account and channel of purchase; web management may open Stripe's customer portal. Confirm that renewal is canceled and check the paid access end date. If a control is unavailable or confirmation is missing, contact support. Signing out, closing a chat, blocking the bot or deleting an account does not by itself confirm cancellation of an external renewal.

    Canceling renewal ordinarily preserves paid access until its stated end. Statutory withdrawal or lawful immediate termination may have different effects. Cancellation does not itself refund earlier payments or erase unused credits. Before changing a plan, review the offered price, effective date and credit treatment; an immediate prorated upgrade is not guaranteed.

    5. Billing corrections and delivery

    Contact us about duplicate or incorrect charges, or confirmed payment without the purchased benefits. We review the payment confirmation and relevant account records and correct verified errors. Any adjustment following a refund, reversal or dispute must be proportionate to the affected purchase; it does not authorize unrelated debits or deletion of unrelated creative work. Support does not replace your provider or statutory dispute rights.

    If a technically failed generation was charged, the corresponding credit charge is restored. If a result was created but is inaccessible, we first seek to restore access to that result without a new generation charge. If recovery is impossible, an appropriate remedy is due, normally restoration of the affected credits and a monetary remedy where law requires it. Subjective dissatisfaction with a delivered AI result differs from technical failure, but mandatory remedies for non-conforming service remain available.

    6. Mandatory consumer rights

    Applicable withdrawal, non-conformity and refund rights remain intact. Depending on the jurisdiction and purchase, these may include supply or correction, a price reduction, termination or a monetary refund. Relevant EU distance contracts may carry a 14-day withdrawal right. Rules differ for ongoing digital services and particular digital content. Early performance and any loss of withdrawal rights require the legally applicable information, express request or consent, acknowledgment and confirmation. General acceptance of Terms, an upfront credit grant or one generation does not automatically waive all rights in an annual service.

    Where law requires an online withdrawal or cancellation function, it must be available in addition to an adequate support route. Canceling future renewal is not necessarily statutory withdrawal. SYLVEX offers no general voluntary refund solely for a change of mind and no general voluntary 14-day money-back guarantee. This does not limit any mandatory remedy.

    7. Requesting a remedy

    Email support@sylvex.ai with the account channel, purchase reference and requested action: renewal cancellation, billing correction, credit restoration or statutory withdrawal. An optional withdrawal statement is: “I withdraw from the contract for [offer], purchased on [date], under [account/receipt].” No particular form is required where law permits another clear statement.

    We verify requests proportionately, explain the outcome and process an owed refund within the applicable legal period. Refunds follow the original payment method where required and feasible; Stars refunds use Telegram's supported mechanism. Bank posting times vary. Stars cashout into fiat is not promised. A refusal or partial decision includes an explanation and available review or redress. Account deletion does not independently create a refund of all previous use.
