    Cookie Policy

    Version: 0.3.0-editorial

    1. Scope

    This policy covers cookies and comparable device storage, including Local Storage and Session Storage, on the website and applicable Mini App surfaces. These technologies can keep you signed in, remember choices, save work and support usage analysis. The Privacy Policy explains associated personal-data processing.

    2. Categories and duration

    Category Purpose Duration
    Account and security Maintain a requested sign-in session and protect access. Session cookies have a default lifetime of up to 30 days, subject to renewal, logout, revocation and browser behavior.
    Preferences Remember language, theme, volume or layout choices. Local preferences may remain until cleared or removed by the application or browser.
    Work and temporary state Preserve drafts, project or editor state, favorites, previews and temporary sign-in steps. Depending on the function, for the tab session or until local data is cleared; browser restoration can affect session storage.
    Usage analytics Recognize visits and understand interaction with sections, models and content. Persistent identifiers may remain until removed or reset, subject to applicable consent and retention rules.
    Visitor and support functions Associate a support interaction with the relevant visit. According to the purpose and applicable choice; optional analytics or marketing is assessed separately from requested messaging.

    Local drafts may include text or media links. Device storage is separate from cloud account records. First-party or pseudonymous identifiers are not automatically necessary or anonymous.

    3. Necessary storage and consent

    Strictly necessary storage may be used for a login or operation you request where law permits. Where prior consent is required for optional tracking, it remains off until you choose it. Optional categories can be accepted or rejected, with no preselected consent, and the choice can later be changed or withdrawn. Terms acceptance is separate.

    Refusing optional tracking does not cancel a paid subscription or remove mandatory rights. Withdrawal affects future consent-based processing and does not invalidate earlier lawful processing.

    4. Managing your choices

    Use the available privacy choices to manage optional categories. You can also manage site data in your browser or supported Telegram WebView settings. Clearing required storage can sign you out or remove local drafts and preferences; it does not itself delete cloud work or cancel a subscription. Save needed local work first and sign out on shared devices.

    Contact support@sylvex.ai about your choices or applicable data rights. A support route does not replace prior consent where consent is legally required.

    5. Third parties and changes

    Selected sign-in or editor features may load resources from third parties, including Google or Telegram. A network request may disclose device or connection information even without a cookie. The Privacy Policy explains recipients and their roles.

    Changes to purposes or technologies require appropriate notice and renewed choice where applicable. See Legal Notices & Policy Updates.
