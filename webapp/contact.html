    2. Information to include

    Identify whether the request concerns the website or Browser Mini App, or the separate Telegram service. Include a safe account reference, a short description and the relevant date or approximate time. Where useful, add a receipt, order or generation reference and a redacted screenshot.

    Never send passwords, login codes or complete card details. Do not attach unnecessary private content or prohibited imagery. We may request proportionate verification through an appropriate route. A support response time is not guaranteed here; applicable legal deadlines remain binding.

    3. Request types

    • Accounts and access: describe the sign-in method and affected account. Exceptional account linking or transfers require verification and approval and are not guaranteed.
    • Purchases and subscriptions: include the purchase channel and receipt reference and state whether you seek cancellation, a correction, credit restoration or a refund. See Payments, Refunds & Cancellation.
    • Privacy and deletion: identify the account and requested scope. Requests remain available while signed out or blocked. See Privacy Policy and Account & Data Deletion.
    • Copyright: identify the work, specific material and your authority, following Intellectual Property & Copyright.
    • Safety or other rights: describe the location and concern without redistributing intimate or child-abuse imagery. See AI Acceptable Use. Contact emergency authorities for an immediate threat to life.
    • Appeals and complaints: identify the decision or conduct, the reason for your objection and the outcome requested.
