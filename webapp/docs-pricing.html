    Pricing & Plans

    Version: 0.3.0-editorial

    1. Temporary equal-access offer

    During the test period, all five subscriptions provide the same access to technically available and permitted models and tools. Prices and included credits differ. Paid generation requires an active qualifying subscription and sufficient available ⚡ credits. Technical capabilities, provider availability and lawful-use restrictions still apply.

    After this period, access to models will be allocated by subscription level, primarily according to model versions, complexity, capabilities and quality. All models will remain visible in the catalog even where a subscription level restricts launching them. The final allocation and transition date have not been announced. We will disclose the applicable rules before they take effect and protect rights already paid for.

    The temporary offer does not guarantee unlimited generations, inclusion of every future model or identical access forever. It introduces no new plan-based limits on characters, storage, concurrent generations or Grid Mode.

    3. Additional credits and usage prices

    The web top-up catalog offers USD 5, 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 100 and 200, plus a custom amount from USD 1 to 1,000, at 100 ⚡ per USD 1. Telegram top-ups use the offered Stars invoices and limits. A top-up adds credits after payment confirmation but does not create or extend a subscription.

    Operation prices depend on the model, output count, resolution, duration, references or metered use. Review the price or disclosed estimate before starting. Included credits are not a fixed number of identical outputs across all models.

    4. Credit treatment and changes

    Credits appear in one balance. Ending subscription access does not by itself erase unused credits, but paid generation requires active qualifying access. Full allocation and charging rules are in Credits & Subscriptions; renewal, cancellation and mandatory remedies are in Payments, Refunds & Cancellation.

    The end of the temporary offer or a future plan structure will not retrospectively reduce already-paid rights or remove granted credits merely to introduce new rules. Material changes identify the affected purchases or renewals and follow Legal Notices & Policy Updates.
